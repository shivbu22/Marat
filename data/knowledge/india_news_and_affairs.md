# Comprehensive India News, Governance, Technology & National Affairs Compendium

## 1. National Governance, Elections & Policy Architecture
- **18th Lok Sabha & Union Government Formation**: Following the nationwide General Elections with over 640 million voters, the 18th Lok Sabha convened with the NDA government prioritizing infrastructure capex, fiscal consolidation toward 4.5% GDP deficit target, tax simplification, and digital public infrastructure expansion.
- **Union Budget Priorities**:
  - Focus on 4 pillars: 'Garib' (Poor), 'Mahilayen' (Women), 'Yuva' (Youth), and 'Annadata' (Farmers).
  - Over ₹11.11 lakh crore allocated to capital expenditure (3.4% of GDP) for highways, railways, defense modernization, and port corridors.
  - Comprehensive review of the Income Tax Act, 1961 for simplified compliance.
  - Employment-Linked Incentive (ELI) schemes with EPFO wage subsidies to spur private formal job creation.
- **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act**: Implementation rules establishing the Data Protection Board of India, consent manager framework, and strict requirements for verifiable parental consent for minors.
- **National Logistics Policy & PM GatiShakti**: Multimodal connectivity platforms reducing logistics costs toward global benchmarks (8-9% of GDP), integrating dedicated freight corridors with port connectivity.

## 2. Technology, Artificial Intelligence & Semiconductor Revolution
- **IndiaAI Mission (₹10,372 Crore Approved)**:
  - National GPU Compute Capacity: Public-private partnership tendering 10,000+ GPUs to provide subsidized AI compute for startups, academia, and research laboratories.
  - IndiaAI Innovation Centre (IAIC): Developing indigenous multi-lingual foundational frontier LLMs and SLMs (Small Language Models) trained on Indian cultural and linguistic diversity.
  - IndiaAI Datasets Platform: Non-personal dataset repository across agriculture, healthcare, climate, and public governance.
  - Sovereign AI Models: Progress by Krutrim (first Indian AI unicorn), Sarvam AI (Sarvam-2B, OpenHathi, Voice AI models), CoRover BharatGPT / Hanooman supporting 22 scheduled Indian languages.
- **India Semiconductor Mission (ISM)**:
  - Tata Electronics & PSMC: ₹91,000 crore semiconductor fabrication plant in Dholera, Gujarat (producing 50,000 wafer starts/month down to 28nm nodes).
  - Tata Semiconductor Assembly and Test (TSAT): ₹27,000 crore OSAT facility in Jagiroad, Morigaon, Assam.
  - CG Power with Renesas (Japan) and Stars Microelectronics (Thailand): ₹7,600 crore OSAT facility in Sanand, Gujarat.
  - Micron Technology: Commercial production ramp-up of the $2.75 billion memory packaging facility in Sanand.
  - Kaynes Semicon: Modern OSAT plant in Sanand, Gujarat for industrial and automotive grade chips.

## 3. ISRO, Space Missions & Deep Technology
- **Gaganyaan (India's Human Spaceflight Programme)**:
  - Astronaut designations announced: Group Captain Prasanth Balakrishnan Nair, Group Captain Ajit Krishnan, Group Captain Angad Prathap, and Wing Commander Shubhanshu Shukla.
  - Uncrewed test flights (Vyommitra humanoid robot) preparing for human orbital launch into 400 km low Earth orbit.
  - Axiom-4 (Ax-4) Mission to the International Space Station (ISS) featuring an Indian astronaut collaboration with NASA/Axiom.
- **Aditya-L1 Solar Mission**: Stationed successfully in halo orbit around Sun-Earth Lagrange Point 1 (L1) ~1.5 million km from Earth, continuously observing coronal mass ejections (CMEs) and solar flare dynamics with SUIT and VELC payloads.
- **Chandrayaan-4 & Chandrayaan-5 Roadmap**: Approved lunar sample-return mission architecture utilizing dual LVM3 launches and autonomous lunar orbit docking.
- **Bharatiya Antariksh Station (BAS)**: First module (BAS-1) scheduled for launch by 2028, with full indigenous space station operational by 2035 and crewed lunar landing targeted by 2040.
- **SpaDeX (Space Docking Experiment)**: Demonstrating autonomous docking in orbit between two spacecraft (Chaser and Target), crucial for human spaceflight and future lunar missions.
- **SSLV Commercialization**: Handing over the Small Satellite Launch Vehicle manufacturing and operations to private industry consortia via NewSpace India Limited (NSIL).

## 4. Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) & Financial Technology
- **Unified Payments Interface (UPI) Globalization**:
  - Live cross-border QR code and P2M / P2P payments in UAE (Mashreq/NEOPAY), Singapore (PayNow-UPI linkage), France (Eiffel Tower & retail), Sri Lanka, Mauritius, Nepal, and Bhutan.
  - Processing over 14 to 15 billion transactions monthly with values exceeding ₹20 lakh crore.
- **Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC)**: Rapid growth beyond food and grocery into mobility, financial credit, mutual funds, insurance, and artisanal exports.
- **Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Monetary Policy**:
  - Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC - Digital Rupee / e₹) pilots scaling to offline retail transactions and interbank programmable credit.
  - Inflation targeting within the 4% (+/-2%) tolerance band, maintaining robust foreign exchange reserves exceeding $650–700 billion.
  - UPI Circle (delegated payments for family members) and UPI 123PAY offline soundbox expansion.

## 5. Clean Energy, Climate & Sustainable Infrastructure
- **PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana (₹75,021 Crore)**:
  - Scheme targeting 1 crore (10 million) households with rooftop solar installations providing up to 300 units of free electricity per month, with central subsidies up to ₹78,000.
- **National Green Hydrogen Mission**:
  - Target: 5 MMT (Million Metric Tonnes) of annual green hydrogen production capacity by 2030, supported by SIGHT (Strategic Interventions for Green Hydrogen Transition) electrolyzer and green ammonia incentives.
- **Renewable Energy Capacity**: Crossing 190+ GW of non-fossil fuel power capacity, with massive solar parks in Khavda (Gujarat, 30 GW planned) and Bhadla (Rajasthan).
- **Railways Modernization**:
  - 100+ Vande Bharat Express routes operational nationwide, introduction of Vande Bharat Sleeper trains for overnight journeys, and Vande Metro for inter-city suburban commutes.
  - Mumbai–Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail (Bullet Train) progress with major river bridges and undersea undersea tunnel excavation in Maharashtra and Gujarat.

## 6. Geopolitics, Strategic Partnerships & Defense Exports
- **Make in India in Defense**:
  - Defense exports reaching record highs of ₹21,083+ crore ($2.6 billion), exporting to over 85 countries including BrahMos supersonic cruise missiles to the Philippines, Pinaka multi-barrel rocket systems, and artillery.
  - Indigenous defense platforms: Tejas Mk1A fighter jet deliveries, INS Vikrant operational deployment, Project 75I submarine acquisition, and Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft (AMCA) 5th-gen fighter clearance.
- **Global Strategic Forums**:
  - Active leadership in Quad (India, US, Japan, Australia), BRICS expansion, G20 troika, and India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC) architecture.
  - Global South voice through Voice of Global South Summits, advocating for sovereign data rights, sustainable development, and inclusive multilateral reform.
